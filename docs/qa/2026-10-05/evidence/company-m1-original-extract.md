[M1-P010] COMPANY NAME: ALING CHONA CAKE AND CUPCAKES

[M1-P011] COMPANY BACKGROUND & OVERVIEW

[M1-P012] Aling Chona Cake & Cupcake was founded on December 30, 2016, by its owner, Chona C. Hinay. The business originally started at the owner's house and gradually developed into a local cake and cupcake business. It specializes in producing cakes and cupcakes for different occasions, including customized orders based on the customer's preferred design, theme, and other specifications.

[M1-P013] The business accepts orders through walk-in transactions and Facebook Messenger. Its general operations involve receiving customer orders, confirming order details, collecting the required down payment, recording orders, preparing the requested cakes or cupcakes, and completing the orders according to the customer's preferred schedule. For customized orders, the customer's design and preferences are communicated through Messenger and used as a reference during preparation.

[M1-P014] VISION, MISSION, AND OBJECTIVES

[M1-P015] Aling Chona Cake & Cupcake operates with a clear focus on providing personalized baked goods to its local community. The business's guiding vision, mission, and operational objectives are stated as follows:

[M1-P016] Vision: To be a trusted and well-known local bakeshop in the community, recognized for making delicious, affordable, and creative customized cakes and cupcakes that make every family celebration special.

[M1-P017] Mission: To bake fresh, high-quality, and personalized cakes and cupcakes that match what each customer wants, while providing friendly service and dependable order preparation for every occasion.

[M1-P018] Objectives:

[M1-P019] Quality Baking: To produce fresh and well-crafted customized cakes and cupcakes tailored to the customer's preferred theme, celebrant's age, and event motif.

[M1-P020] Timely Fulfillment: To prepare and release customer orders on time based on their agreed pickup or delivery schedule.

[M1-P021] Customer Satisfaction: To maintain good customer relationships through responsive communication on Facebook Messenger and fair, transparent pricing.

[M1-P022] Operational Improvement: To improve daily business operations by maintaining clear and organized records of customer orders, payments, and baking supplies.

[M1-P023] ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

[M1-P024] Aling Chona Cake & Cupcake operates as a small, home-based local business run by two individuals: the owner and an assistant. Because of its small scale, the enterprise maintains a simple, direct hierarchy without separate corporate departments:

[M1-P025] Owner / Head Baker (Chona C. Hinay): The owner oversees the entire business. She manages customer inquiries, confirms orders through Facebook Messenger and walk-in visits, sets product prices, and collects down payments. As the head baker, she personally prepares and decorates customized cakes according to the customer's requested theme, age, or motif. She is also responsible for overall financial decisions and key supply purchases.

[M1-P026] Assistant / Support Staff: The assistant helps the owner with daily operational tasks. The assistant assists in the kitchen with basic baking preparation, runs errands to purchase and restock ingredients from nearby stores when supplies run low, updates order progress, logs completed orders in the notebook, and assists customers when they arrive to pick up their orders.

[M1-P027] PRODUCTS / SERVICES OFFERED

[M1-P028] Aling Chona Cake & Cupcake offers baked goods and customized baking services tailored for various events such as birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, and community celebrations:

[M1-P029] 1. Products Offered:

[M1-P030] Customized Cakes: Special occasion cakes designed and decorated according to customer requests. Customers can specify design themes, the celebrant's age, and color motifs (such as for weddings or milestone events).

[M1-P031] Cupcakes: Freshly baked cupcakes prepared for parties, events, or giveaway treats, which can also be customized to match the theme of a celebration cake.

[M1-P032] 2. Services Offered:

[M1-P033] Custom Design and Consultation: Customers can send sample photos, preferred themes, and decoration ideas through Facebook Messenger or share them in person to plan their custom cake design.

[M1-P034] Advance Order and Reservation: The business accepts advance bookings with a 50% down payment to reserve specific dates for upcoming celebrations.

[M1-P035] Scheduled Pickup and Local Delivery Coordination: Completed orders are scheduled for customer pickup at an agreed date and time, with local delivery arrangements coordinated when requested.

[M1-P036] CURRENT BUSINESS PROCESSES OVERVIEW

[M1-P037] Aling Chona Cake & Cupcake currently manages its daily operations through a combination of walk-in transactions and online orders through Facebook Messenger. The main operational processes include the following:

[M1-P038] 1. Order Placement and Inquiry: Customers contact the business either by walking into the shop or by sending a message through Facebook Messenger. Customers provide their preferred cake or cupcake design, size, flavor, theme, celebrant's age, or wedding motif, along with their desired pickup date and time.

[M1-P039] 2. Order Confirmation and Down Payment: The owner checks the baking schedule and confirms the order with the customer via Messenger. To secure the order, the customer is required to pay a 50% down payment, which is paid either through GCash or in cash. Once paid, the owner confirms the payment and provides a receipt (or sends a photo of the receipt through Messenger).

[M1-P040] 3. Order Recording and Supply Preparation: Once an order is confirmed, the owner writes the customer's order details, pickup date, and remaining balance into a physical notebook. To keep ingredients fresh and avoid spoilage, the business does not stock large quantities of supplies. Ingredients are purchased on a weekly basis or as needed for incoming orders. If specific ingredients run low unexpectedly, the assistant or owner quickly purchases them from a nearby local store.

[M1-P041] 4. Production and Cake Preparation: The owner and assistant prepare the ingredients and bake the cakes or cupcakes. The owner uses the customer's design preferences and messages in Messenger as a direct guide during decorating to ensure the finished cake matches the requested theme. Order progress is monitored manually by checking the handwritten notes in the notebook.

[M1-P042] 5. Order Claiming, Final Payment, and Record-Keeping: When the order is ready, the customer is notified for pickup. The customer settles the remaining 50% balance in cash or via GCash upon receiving the cake. Finally, the owner or assistant writes the completed transaction (customer name and completion date) into the business logbook.

[M1-P043] CURRENT IT ENVIRONMENT

[M1-P044] Aling Chona Cake & Cupcake currently relies on basic, everyday technology to support its daily business operations. The current technical setup includes the following:

[M1-P045] Hardware: The business uses personal mobile smartphones for customer communications, receiving design photos, and verifying online payments. A basic handheld calculator is used for simple math calculations. The business does not use desktop computers, laptops, POS (Point of Sale) machines, or barcode scanners.

[M1-P046] Communication and Social Media: Facebook Messenger is the primary digital communication tool used by the business. It allows the owner to receive customer inquiries, discuss custom cake designs, receive photo references, confirm orders, and send photos of receipts for received down payments.

[M1-P047] Digital Payments: GCash is used as an electronic payment channel. This allows customers to send their 50% down payment remotely and settle their final balances conveniently upon pickup.

[M1-P048] Manual Record-Keeping Limitations: The business does not use any spreadsheets (such as Microsoft Excel), accounting software, or computerized inventory and order tracking systems. All customer orders, pickup dates, and remaining balances are written by hand in a notebook. Completed transactions are recorded in a paper logbook that only lists the customer's name and completion date, without recording item prices, daily total sales, expenses, or profit margins. In addition, ingredient restocking is done without digital inventory tracking.

[M1-P049] Overall, while the business uses smartphones, Facebook Messenger, and GCash for convenient communication and payment collection, all internal records, order monitoring, sales tracking, and inventory management remain entirely manual.
